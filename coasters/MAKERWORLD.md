> Pipe your coffee to /dev/null. It won't leak.

Every Unix machine has a black hole. Now your mug has somewhere to sit.

This coaster is based on the logo of /dev/null, the social network for developers. You sign in with GitHub and commit
your messages to the log instead of tweeting them.

## Why you need one

- No top surface: the Hilbert curve infill is the texture. Your slicer finally gets to show off.
- 0 bytes, 0 dependencies. It never needs an update, only a refill.
- Prints in one go. No supports, no glue.

## What's in the 3MF

- 2 coasters, one per plate: dark or light
- Plates ready to slice, colors and per-part settings already assigned
