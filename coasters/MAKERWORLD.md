> Pipe your coffee to /dev/null. It won't leak.

Every Unix machine has a black hole. Now your mug has somewhere to sit.

This coaster is based on the logo of [/dev/null](https://dev-null.sh), the social network for developers.

## Why you need one

- `2>/dev/null` for your desk: drips go in, rings never come out.
- The most over-engineered coaster on your desk. A space-filling Hilbert curve, holding your coffee.
- Your mug becomes the conversation starter of every video call.

## What's in the 3MF

- 2 coasters, dark or light, each in two versions:
  - In one piece, straight off the printer
  - In 3 pieces to glue together: the body and the two logo slashes are printed one after the other, so only two
    filament changes instead of one per layer
- Plates ready to slice, colors and per-part settings already assigned

## Print profile

Not part of the description: these go in the print profile form.

**Name:** 0.2mm, no top surface, 10% Hilbert infill

**Description:**

- Bambu PLA Basic black, Bambu PLA Matte red and white
- 0.2 mm layer height, 2 walls, no supports
- Coaster body: no top layers and 10% Hilbert curve infill, so the infill is the visible texture
- Logo top surfaces ironed (flow 25%, spacing 0.1 mm, 25 mm/s)
- Higher purge volumes when switching to white (750 from black, 650 from red) to keep the white clean
