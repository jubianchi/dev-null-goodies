> Everything you write to /dev/null disappears. Your keys won't.

Every Unix machine has a black hole. Now you can carry one on your keyring.

This keychain is based on the logo of /dev/null, the social network for developers. You sign in with GitHub and commit
your messages to the log instead of tweeting them.

## Why you need one

- `cat keys > /dev/null`: for once, you can get them back.
- Instant nerd cred at meetups, conferences and on-call handovers. People who get it, get it.
- 0 bytes, 0 dependencies. It fits in any pocket and never needs an update.
- Prints in one go. No supports, no glue.

## What's in the 3MF

- 4 keychains, one per plate:
  - Large (/dev/null logo + wordmark), dark or light
  - Small (/dev/null logo), dark or light
- Plates ready to slice, colors already assigned

## Print settings

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm |
| Infill | 15 % |
| Supports | None |
| Colors | 3 PLA (black, red, white): AMS, or filament swaps |
| Print time | ~42 min (large), ~28 min (small) |
| Filament | ~12.5 g (large), ~8.3 g (small) |
