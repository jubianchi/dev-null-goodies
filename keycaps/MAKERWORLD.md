> Your Escape and Backspace keys never felt so honest.

Every Unix machine has a black hole. Now your keyboard has a key for it.

These keycaps are based on the logo of [/dev/null](https://dev-null.sh), the social network for developers.

## Why you need one

- `rm -rf` your typos in style: a Backspace that pipes them straight to /dev/null.
- Escape is the only way out of vim. Make it the most beautiful key on your board.
- The logo is printed in the first layers, inlaid in the top: it will outlive your keyboard switches.

## What's in the 3MF

- 1u keycaps (Escape or any other key) and 2u keycaps (Backspace), dark or light
- The 2u comes with the mark and the wordmark (0.2 mm nozzle), or with the mark only (0.4 mm nozzle)
- A clearance test coupon, to tune the stem fit to your printer before printing the keycaps
- Cherry MX stems, and Cherry stabilizer stems on the 2u

## Print profile

Not part of the description: these go in the print profile form.

**Name:** 0.08mm, 3 colors, flush first layer

**Description:**

- Bambu PLA Basic: black, red, white
- Printed upside down, top face on a textured PEI plate: no supports
- 0.2 mm first layer, then 0.08 mm, 2 walls, 15% gyroid infill
- 1u and 2u mark only: 0.4 mm nozzle; 2u mark and wordmark: 0.2 mm nozzle
- First layer with one wall, 25% infill/wall overlap, 30 mm/s walls and 50 mm/s infill, no elephant foot compensation, so the color regions meet flush
- Higher purge volumes when switching to white (750 from black, 650 from red) to keep the white clean
- Print the clearance coupon first: the 0.05 mm default is validated on a Bambu Lab H2S
