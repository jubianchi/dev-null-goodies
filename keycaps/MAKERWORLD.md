> Your Escape and Backspace keys never felt so honest.

Every Unix machine has a black hole. Now your keyboard has a key for it.

These keycaps are based on the logo of [/dev/null](https://dev-null.sh), the social network for developers.

## Why you need one

- `rm -rf` your typos in style: a Backspace that pipes them straight to /dev/null.
- Escape is the only way out of vim. Make it the most beautiful key on your board.
- The logo is printed in the first layers, inlaid in the top: it will outlive your keyboard switches.

## What's in the 3MF

- 4 keycaps: 1u (Escape or any other key) and 2u (Backspace), dark or light
- A clearance test coupon, to tune the stem fit to your printer before printing the keycaps
- Cherry MX stems, and Cherry stabilizer stems on the 2u
